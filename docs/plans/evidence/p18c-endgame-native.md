# P18c — Native endgame entry, recording and settlement

Local unpublished integration following save20 commit `52ad707`. The ordinary
recorded-wave receipt remains the sole recording/publication/settlement
boundary. Immutable area/endgame origins separate dungeon authority from
Act V/Normal reward context. Native `startEndgameRun` uses issued dungeon
plans/seeds, manual mode and its own durable ordinal; it accepts no caller
seed, level, replay profile or campaign counter.

Entry captures exact durable before/after references. Publication observes
those references before metadata, after the setter/selection and during final
recognition, including replay and later-wave installation. Throw-before-write
can recover; throw-after-write recognizes the captured state without another
simulation, ordinal increment or Iron payment. Foreign references enter
conflict and are preserved. Every failed entry attempt updates the observed
receipt while retaining the first diagnostic. Predecessor receipts retain the
committed state, so replay/staging retries cannot adopt an equal foreign copy.

Native original-member victory drives progression. Ordinary income and the
final flawless-gem cache/count/progress share the existing settlement rollback.
Next-wave staging can retry after accepted income without paying it again.
Dungeon rewards cannot publish campaign/Nightmare clear, quest/monster/boss
credit or idle-pity changes. A screen transition adapter is available; the
player-facing endgame controls are not part of this slice.
