or raw marker cannot attest authority. The spirit never schedules ordinary

summons, including the spirit, during damage reads without rewriting base stats.

the real spirit; increased life capacity does not heal.

Defense 10 reuse the existing generic summon baseline as explicit provisional

changes apply next wave; original-resource replay does not duplicate income.

requirements, previews base stats before combat bonuses and localizes the real
