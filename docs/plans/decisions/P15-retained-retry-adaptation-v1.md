## Selected population boundary

Retain all 14 builds × five acts × three roles, including the five historical
failed branches, eleven noncanonical contexts and Double Throw's distinct
lineage. No outcome-based source substitution is allowed. Each selection uses
`context.retainedBaseline.source` and its matching extraction, never the

order, aura, mercenary, formation or resource preparation. Start at its whole-area
boundary with its full saved Normal ordinal and timestamp. Seven historical
pre-Baal wave boundaries remain internal boundaries; their selected original
whole-area starts remain the input. Native admission owns refill and all prefix
carry. A role blocked by a failed prefix is still unplayed; a missing historical
plan is still unknown.

## After each terminal nonwin

The complete per-context `declaration` can be passed to the existing
`measureCampaignRetries` function after authenticating and hydrating its selected
source once. It sets `maximumAttempts: 3` and `equipment: "unchanged"`.

1. Visit already learned signature skills in the pinned identity's
   `signatureByAct` order, targeting their authored cap up to rank 20. Then visit
   already learned support skills in the pinned growth order, collapsing repeats
   to their greatest declared rank. Include only skills already learned at the
   source, eligible at its level, with all prerequisites learned there. Do not
   unlock later targets when a retry grants a new level. Targets below an
   existing rank cause no change; they never reduce investment.
2. Use currently owned skill points at the native cost of one per increment.
   Spend complete five-stat-point bundles using the selected build's explicit
   `statsPerLevel`; preserve the remainder and all previous investment. There
   are no stage rewrites or refunds. Native caps/refusals remain enforced;
   unexpected refusal preserves the journal and live successor, never a free
   restart or a clamped result.
3. Preserve the exact selected source's combo order. The request has no reset,
   progression-preparer, conditional-combo, aura or equipment-policy flags.
   Equipment, fielded mercenary and formation remain as retained. No farming,
   purchasing, crafting, socketing, recruitment, respec, grant or resource edit
   is permitted between attempts. Depleted budgets may produce no adaptation.

This policy specializes existing builds instead of applying the same generic
recovery allocation to every class. It makes no claim that these choices meet
the ≥95% within-three success target. If later evaluation rejects them, retain
that result and record a new policy version before a distinct evaluation;
do not reinterpret old trials using a changed policy.

## Execution and measurement

The existing runner preserves contiguous native attempts. It returns through
the ordinary terminal recovery boundary, retains earned income and once-only
quest settlement, advances the Normal ordinal once per admission modulo 360360,
and advances the logical clock by the ceiling of every played wave's terminal
milliseconds, including losses. Native seeds derive from canonical area and
actual admission time. Stop at first complete living-hero victory or attempt
three. Never restore between attempts or cherry-pick independent victories.

The manifest is a declaration cohort, not 210 executed sequences or a statistical
seed population. No new sample keys, weights or independent-seed claims are
selected here. The exact source timestamp/ordinal specifies a possible native
sequence, not a population success rate. Existing checkpoint sample partitions
are unchanged; do not silently relabel these clock-derived native seeds as
checkpoint sample seeds. Manual/idle, ordinal/pity, recommended power and earned
acquisition populations still need their own prospective resolution. Historical
unknown acquisition costs remain unknown. Combat-only accounting cannot prove
30–50 active-hour pacing.
